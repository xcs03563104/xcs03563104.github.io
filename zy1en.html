Please Do Not Use My Art as References

Published at 26/2/28

Recently, I have noticed that someone has been using my artwork as references for commissions, and even asking others to follow the style of my pieces.
The illustrations I created feature someone else's original characters, but the paintings themselves are my own work.
If you come across similar images, please be aware that the original artist is me.
I do not want my creations to be used as templates for other commissions without credit
